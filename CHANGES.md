# What changed

Changes to the factory dashboard, newest first, in plain words. The dashboard's "What changed" page
shows this list. Keep each line short, and add new lines at the top of their day.

## 18 October 2026

- The chat answers questions about cost and time, from the same figures the pages show.
- You can ask the chat by voice: hold the microphone, speak, and hear a short answer read aloud.
- Feature and project pages show what each build cost (tokens, calls and a nominal price) and how long it took.
- A client account sees only its own projects, and nothing at all if its projects are not set up.
- The chat answers questions from the dashboard's own records, with links to the pages they come from.
- The dashboard has the Appmilla look: new colours, fonts and logo, a dark mode, and a tab bar on phones.
- The pages show live data from the factory's record, and say plainly when it is out of date or missing.
- Everyone signs in with their own account and password.

## 17 October 2026

- The dashboard reads the factory's live build record, and cannot change it.
